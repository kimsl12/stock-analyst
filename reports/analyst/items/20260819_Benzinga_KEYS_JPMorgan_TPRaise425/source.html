These Analysts Boost Their Forecasts On Keysight Following Upbeat Q3 Results

출처: Benzinga, 2026-08-19

원문 링크: benzinga.com

핵심 인용 (발췌)

Keysight Technologies reported better-than-expected third-quarter results, with adjusted earnings of $3.07 per share (beating the $2.48 analyst consensus estimate) and quarterly revenue of $1.85 billion (beating the $1.74 billion estimate).
J.P. Morgan increased its price target on Keysight to $425 from $400 while keeping an Overweight rating. UBS adjusted the price target to $440 from $420, maintaining a Buy rating.
Management forecasting revenue of $1.93 billion to $1.95 billion (well above the $1.82 billion analyst consensus) and adjusted EPS of $3.34 to $3.40 (compared with Wall Street expectations of approximately $2.68 per share). Demand for testing solutions linked to artificial intelligence infrastructure was a major contributor to the increase.
