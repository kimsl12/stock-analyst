GLJ리서치, GE버노바(GEV) 신규 커버리지 개시 — 매도, 목표주가 470달러

출처: 24/7 Wall St. | 발행일: 2026-09-14 | 애널리스트: GLJ Research

GLJ Research initiated coverage of GE Vernova (NYSE:GEV) with a Sell rating and a Street-low $470 price target, roughly 46.9% below the stock's price at initiation. GLJ described the company as "a cyclical gas turbine manufacturer priced as a secular compounder." Shares fell 8.6%-9% on the day of the call. The GRID infrastructure ETF fell approximately 4% in sympathy versus the S&P 500's 0.3% decline, with Eaton (ETN, -7%) and Quanta Services (PWR, -4%) also dropping.
