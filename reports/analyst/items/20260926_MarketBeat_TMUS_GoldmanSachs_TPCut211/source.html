Goldman Sachs Adjusts Price Target on T-Mobile US to $211 From $230, Keeps Buy Rating

출처: MarketBeat, 2026-09-26

The Goldman Sachs Group lowered their target price on T-Mobile US from $230.00 to $211.00 and set a "buy" rating on the stock in a report on Friday (September 26, 2026). Analysts cited competitive pressure, macroeconomic risks and T-Mobile's roughly $84.6 billion of debt. Despite the price target cut, Goldman Sachs maintained its positive stance with the buy rating. TMUS stock headed for its worst week in six years, but Goldman still sees roughly 35% upside.

한글 정리

Goldman Sachs가 T모바일 목표가를 230달러에서 211달러로 낮추면서도 '매수' 등급은 유지했다. 경쟁 압박, 거시경제 리스크, 약 846억달러 규모 부채 부담이 하향 근거로 꼽혔다. T모바일 주가는 최근 6년래 최악의 주간 낙폭을 기록했지만 Goldman은 여전히 약 35%의 상승 여력이 있다고 판단했다.
