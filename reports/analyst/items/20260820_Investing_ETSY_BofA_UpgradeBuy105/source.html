Why is Etsy stock surging today?

출처: Investing.com, 2026-08-20 11:22

원문 링크: investing.com

핵심 인용 (발췌)

BofA Securities upgraded Etsy to Buy from Neutral and raised its price target to $105 from $88, citing "durable execution under CEO Kruti Patel Goyal's four strategic growth pillars." The firm described the company's full-year outlook as conservative based on third-party data, and highlighted that the mobile app drives 47% of gross merchandise sales, a "crucial driver of higher buyer lifetime value and enhanced personalization."
Separately, Rosenblatt initiated coverage with a Buy rating and a $95 target, citing that "the recovery in gross merchandise sales has reached a true turning point," while Goldman Sachs reinstated coverage at Neutral with an $89 target, describing the company as "still in an execution phase." Etsy stock surged 3.9% in pre-market trading following the wave of analyst actions, alongside Q2 2026 earnings of $0.98 adjusted EPS versus $0.73 consensus.
