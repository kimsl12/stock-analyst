RBC, 로슈 신규 커버리지 개시 — 아웃퍼폼(매수), 목표주가 400스위스프랑

출처: ad-hoc-news.de | 발행일: 2026-09-09 | 애널리스트: RBC Capital Markets / UBS / Bernstein

RBC Capital initiated coverage of Roche (ROG) with an Outperform rating and a CHF 400.00 price target, highlighting "underappreciated pipeline potential" alongside core business strength. UBS reaffirmed its Buy rating with a CHF 376.00 target, citing a strong Q2 2026 where pharma revenue "exceeded consensus expectations by about 1 percent," with oncology drugs Hemlibra, Tecentriq, and Perjeta outperforming internally. Bernstein raised its target to CHF 434.50, arguing that "improved research and development productivity and a promising pipeline of upcoming events" justified the higher valuation. Roche shares have delivered 29.7% returns over the past year, trading between the 52-week low (CHF 252.20) and recent high (CHF 375.80).
