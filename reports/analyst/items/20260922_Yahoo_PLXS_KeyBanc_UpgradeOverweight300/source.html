키뱅크, 플렉서스 '비중확대'로 상향 — 목표가 300달러

원본 출처 (Yahoo Finance): https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/netflix-downgraded-sandisk-initiated-wall-134453114.html

KeyBanc upgraded Plexus (PLXS) to Overweight from Sector Weight with a $300 price target following investor meetings. The call was part of Yahoo Finance's Tuesday, September 22, 2026 roundup of Wall Street's top analyst calls.
