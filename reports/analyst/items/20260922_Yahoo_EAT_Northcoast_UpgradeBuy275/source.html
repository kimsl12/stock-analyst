원본 출처 (Yahoo Finance): https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/netflix-downgraded-sandisk-initiated-wall-134453114.html

Northcoast upgraded Brinker (EAT) to Buy from Neutral with a $275 price target, believing the company's Chili's unit is positioned for long-term growth under new management. The call was part of Yahoo Finance's Tuesday, September 22, 2026 roundup of Wall Street's top analyst calls.
