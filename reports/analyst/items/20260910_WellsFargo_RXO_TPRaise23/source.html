웰스파고, RXO 목표주가 22→23달러로 상향 — 이퀄웨이트(중립) 유지

출처: Investing.com | 발행일: 2026-09-10 | 애널리스트: Wells Fargo

Wells Fargo raised its price target on RXO to $23.00 from $22.00 while maintaining an Equal Weight rating, citing "improving brokerage dynamics in the third quarter." The firm noted that "lower spot rates and improved contractual pricing is driving higher gross profit per load," and observed that larger brokers are gaining market share, expecting this trend to accelerate through 2027 due to increased liability concerns in the industry. Similar positive developments were observed across comparable companies including CH Robinson and ArcBest Asset Light. As of the article date, RXO traded at $20.04, having surged 58.54% year-to-date.
