RBC, 사노피 신규 커버리지 개시 — 섹터퍼폼(중립), 목표주가 80유로

출처: ad-hoc-news.de | 발행일: 2026-09-09 | 애널리스트: RBC Capital Markets (Trung Huynh)

RBC Capital initiated coverage of Sanofi (SAN) with a Sector Perform rating and a price target of EUR 80.00, framing the call around a "balanced risk reward profile" for the French pharmaceutical company while highlighting "transition risks in Sanofi's portfolio." The target sits roughly 7.9% above the then-current trading price of EUR 74.13. The consensus analyst target stands at EUR 94.45 across 24 analysts, suggesting 26.4% upside potential, while legal proceedings in India add regulatory uncertainty to the investment backdrop.
