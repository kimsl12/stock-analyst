JP모건, 바클레이스 목표주가 610→620펜스로 상향 — 비중확대 유지

출처: MarketBeat (Digital Look 리서치 노트 인용) | 발행일: 2026-09-15 | 애널리스트: JPMorgan Chase & Co.

JPMorgan Chase & Co. raised their price target on Barclays (LON:BARC) from GBX 610 to GBX 620 and gave the stock an "Overweight" rating in a research report issued to investors on Tuesday. The new target implies an upside of 30.75% from the previous close. The broader analyst consensus rates Barclays as a "Moderate Buy" with an average target of GBX 578.33 across six Buy and one Hold rating.
