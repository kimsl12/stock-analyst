JPMorgan Chase & Co. Cuts Elbit Systems (NASDAQ:ESLT) Price Target to $885.00

출처: MarketBeat, 2026-08-19

원문 링크: marketbeat.com

핵심 인용 (발췌)

JPMorgan Chase & Co. decreased their price target on Elbit Systems from $950.00 to $885.00 and set a "neutral" rating on the stock in a report on Wednesday (August 19, 2026).
Elbit Systems reported stronger-than-expected second-quarter 2026 results, with non-GAAP earnings of $4.14 a share on revenue of $2.3 billion, beating Wall Street estimates of $3.24 a share and $2.22 billion, respectively. Revenue rose 15.9% from a year earlier, while backlog reached a record $32 billion. Despite the strong Q2 earnings beat, the stock fell 8.43% to $774.73 in pre-market trading, suggesting investors were looking past the beat and focusing on valuation, taxes and the pace at which the company can convert orders into sales.
