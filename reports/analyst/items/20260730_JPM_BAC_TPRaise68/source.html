Bank of America (NYSE:BAC) Trading 1.1% Higher Following Analyst Upgrade

출처: Daily Political · 2026-07-30 · 원문 링크

원문 발췌 (핵심 문장)

JPMorgan Chase & Co. raised their price target on Bank of America stock from $62.50 to $68.00, with the stock rising 1.1% following the upgrade. Multiple other analysts also issued positive ratings around the same period, including Barclays, Truist Financial, Jefferies, and Wells Fargo, all maintaining buy or overweight ratings with targets ranging from $64–$75.
