## Top 5 Risks (Detail)

### 1. AI CapEx Cycle Peak-Out
Description: Hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending could plateau or decline in 2027-2028
as ROI on AI investments comes under scrutiny.
Probability: MEDIUM - Past tech cycles (cloud 2019, crypto mining 2018) show CapEx
cycles last 3-4 years. Current AI cycle started 2023. Peak-out in 2027 is plausible.
Impact: -20% to -35% (revenue miss + multiple compression double-hit)
Trigger: Any hyperscaler cutting or flattening CapEx guidance
Mitigant: Inference demand growing structurally (60-65% of compute), sovereign AI new demand

### 2. US-China Export Controls
Description: Further tightening of chip export controls could eliminate ~15% of revenue.
China is developing alternatives (Huawei Ascend) which could permanently displace NVIDIA.
Probability: MEDIUM - Current H200 case-by-case is fragile. Section 301 investigation
on 15 countries including forced labor/overcapacity. 50% tariff threat if China-Iran link.
Impact: -10% to -15% revenue (already partially priced in)
Trigger: Summit failure (May 14) or new BIS Entity List additions
Mitigant: Busan agreement eased tensions; China rev already declining from 25% to 15%

### 3. ASIC Competition
Description: Custom AI chips (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Broadcom/Marvell ASIC) could
take significant share in inference workloads, the fastest-growing segment.
Probability: MEDIUM - Google and Amazon actively deploying custom chips internally.
Broadcom AI ASIC TAM guided at 00-900B by 2027. This is happening, not hypothetical.
Impact: -5% to -10% revenue over 2-3 years (gradual share erosion in inference)
Trigger: Hyperscaler announces >50% of inference on ASIC
Mitigant: CUDA ecosystem makes GPU switching costly. Training still GPU-dominated.

### 4. Valuation Compression
Description: Fwd PER 30.7x assumes 37%+ EPS growth. If growth decelerates to 20%,
PER could compress to 20-25x, implying -20% to -35% downside.
Probability: MEDIUM - Law of large numbers: 80B revenue base makes 30%+ growth
increasingly difficult. Historical precedent: growth deceleration always triggers de-rating.
Impact: -20% to -35%
Trigger: FY27 Q1 revenue or guidance below consensus
Mitigant: PEG 0.82 provides buffer. Buybacks (5B/yr) support EPS even if growth slows.

### 5. TSMC Supply Concentration
Description: NVIDIA is 100% dependent on TSMC for advanced node manufacturing.
Taiwan geopolitical risk (China military exercises) could disrupt supply.
Probability: LOW-MEDIUM - No imminent military action, but exercises increasing.
TSMC Arizona fab mitigates partially but Taiwan still 80%+ of capacity.
Impact: Very High (-30% to -50% in disruption scenario)
Trigger: Major military escalation in Taiwan Strait
Mitigant: TSMC Arizona fab, CHIPS Act subsidies, inventory buffer

## Worst Case Scenario
