## 3. Growth Assessment

### Organic Growth
- Data Center: Blackwell ramp (87% YoY in FY26, 30-40% expected FY27)
- Inference market expansion: 60-65% of AI compute shifting to inference
- Networking revenue: InfiniBand + Ethernet growing 50%+ (post-Mellanox)
- Software/Platform: CUDA Enterprise, NIM microservices = recurring revenue
- Sovereign AI: 30+ countries building national AI infra with NVIDIA GPUs

### New Markets
- Automotive: DRIVE Thor (FY26 +52%) - autonomous driving platform
- Robotics: Isaac platform, humanoid robots
- Digital twins: Omniverse for industrial simulation
- Edge AI: Jetson series for IoT/embedded

## Key Takeaways
1. AI infra build-out is the defining megatrend. NVIDIA is the primary beneficiary
2. Porter score 14/25: Attractive but TSMC dependency and ASIC competition are real
3. Pricing power extraordinary (OPM 64% vs peer 25%) due to CUDA ecosystem
4. Growth visibility 2 years: Blackwell -> Rubin -> next-gen cadence
5. Key risk: ASIC share in inference. If ASIC > 30% by 2028, growth model challenged
